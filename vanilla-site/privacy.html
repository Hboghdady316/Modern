Privacy

How this site handles inquiries, booking links, and basic site metrics.

Inquiries

The inquiry form uses the details you provide to deliver your message to Haitham for a response. Only include information you want to share about your inquiry.

Bookings and sign-in

If a meeting booking link is available, it opens the scheduling provider, whose privacy terms apply. The owner area uses a session cookie for sign-in.

Site metrics

We count aggregate Work views, CV clicks, contact actions and delivered inquiries without analytics cookies or individual visitor profiles. Browser privacy signals are respected.
